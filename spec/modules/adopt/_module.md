Bringing an existing codebase under a spec: units drafted from evidence, the list of what still needs a person, and confirming drafts.

## Owns
- Draft markers: `draft: true` with `sources:` on frontmatter units, `· Draft: <source>` on contracts and invariants
- `spec gaps [--no-tests]`
- `spec confirm <ID>...`

## Non-goals
- Writing the drafts: that is the `/spec-init` command, run by an agent
