When the fallback provider also rate-limits, the app moves on to a third provider.
Steps: set a fallback, make the active provider rate-limit, then make the fallback rate-limit as well.
Expected: the reply stops with stopReason "rate_limited" and the fallback's retry delay.
Actual: the conversation is sent to yet another enabled provider.
